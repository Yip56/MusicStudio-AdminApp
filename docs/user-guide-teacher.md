# StudioOS — Teacher Guide

This guide shows you how to use StudioOS as a teacher: check your timetable, mark attendance, look at your students, and ask for a grade change.

You can only see and change **your own** classes. Other teachers' classes appear as "Occupied" so you know a room is busy.

---

## 1. Logging in

1. Open StudioOS in your web browser (or on your phone).
2. Click **Sign in with Google**.
   ![The sign-in screen](TODO)
3. Choose the Gmail account the studio registered for you.
4. You will land on your **Calendar**.

**If you see "Access Denied":** you signed in with a Gmail account the studio has not registered. Click **Sign out and try another account**, or ask your studio administrator to invite the Gmail address you use.

To log out, click **Sign Out** at the bottom of the left menu (on a phone: open **More**, then **Sign Out**).

---

## 2. Viewing my schedule

1. Click **Calendar** in the menu.
2. Pick a date on the month grid. The small number on a date is how many classes are booked that day.
   ![The calendar with a day selected](TODO)
3. The day is shown as one column per room. Each class card shows the time, the student's name, the instrument and the teacher.
4. Cards that say **Occupied** belong to another teacher. You cannot open them.

### A student who learns two instruments

A student who learns two instruments has **two separate classes**, each with its own teacher, room and time. They appear **twice** in the calendar — once for each instrument, at that class's own time.

For example, Mia learns Piano on Saturday at 09:00 and Drum on Saturday at 11:00. You will see "Mia · Piano" at 09:00 and "Mia · Drum" at 11:00. You only see the one that is yours.

## 3. Marking attendance

1. On the **Calendar**, pick the date of the class.
2. Click the class card. A small window opens.
   ![The attendance window](TODO)
3. Click **Present** or **Absent**.
4. To undo a mistake, open the card again and click the same button again. The class goes back to "Not marked".

Mark each instrument separately for a student who has two classes on the same day.

### If a student misses a class and you want to schedule a make-up

This is available for students on a normal (full-fee) plan.

1. Open the class card and click **Defer Class**.
2. Enter the make-up date, and optionally a start and end time.
3. Click **Schedule Makeup**.
4. The make-up class shows on that date with a purple "Makeup" label. To cancel it, open it and click **Remove Deferral** (or **× Remove makeup**).

### Booking an extra, one-off class

1. On the **Calendar**, pick the date.
2. Under the room you want, click **+ Book Session**.
3. Choose the student and instrument, then the start and end time, then click **Check & Book**.
4. If the booking is not allowed you will see the reason, for example:
   - "This student already has another class at that time."
   - "This teacher is already teaching at that time."
   - "This room is not set up for [instrument]."
5. If the room is already taken by another teacher's student, you are offered **Send Clash Request**. Your booking waits as "Pending" until that teacher approves it.

---

## 4. Viewing my students and their instruments

1. Click **Students** in the menu.
2. You see one row for every class you teach: student, instrument, grade, day, time and fee type.
   ![The students list](TODO)
3. Use the search box to find a student by name or instrument.
4. A student with two instruments shows a purple **2 instruments** label. Only the instruments you teach are listed for you.
5. Click **Show stopped** to include students who have finished lessons with the studio.

---

## 5. Updating a grade

Teachers **ask** for a grade change; the administrator approves it. You cannot change a grade yourself.

1. Click **Students**.
2. Find the right student **and the right instrument** (a student with two instruments has a row for each).
3. Click **Request Grade**.
   ![The grade request window](TODO)
4. Choose the new grade, write a short reason, and click **Send Request**.
5. The administrator is notified. When they approve it, the grade on the student's row changes.

Good to know:

- If the studio has already collected this month's fee, the fee that was collected **does not change** — an approved grade only affects later fees. The administrator is reminded of this when they approve or edit a grade.
- There is no automatic "lock". Your request can always be sent.

---

## 6. Requests from other teachers (the bell)

The 🔔 bell shows requests waiting for you. The most common one is a **Room Clash Request**: another teacher wants a room at a time when your student is booked.

1. Click the 🔔 bell.
2. Read the request.
3. Click **Approve** to let them use the room, or **Reject** to keep your booking.

Requests you have answered move to **Archive → Notification Archive**, so you can look them up later.

---

## 7. Commission and pay

Payroll, fees and commission are handled by the studio administrator. **You do not see payroll, fees or commission screens in StudioOS.** If you have a question about your pay or commission rate, please ask your administrator.

---

## 8. Common problems and fixes

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Access Denied" after signing in | You used a Gmail the studio doesn't know. Sign out and use the registered one, or ask the administrator to invite your address. |
| I can't see a class I teach | Check the date. The class only shows on its weekday, and only after the student's start date. Ask the administrator to check the student's enrolment is not stopped. |
| A card says "Occupied" and I can't open it | It belongs to another teacher. That is normal. |
| I can't mark a class | Only your own classes can be marked. Make sure you opened your card, not an "Occupied" one. |
| I marked Present by mistake | Open the card and click **Present** again to undo it, then choose the right button. |
| My booking says the student or teacher is already busy | Pick a different time. A student and a teacher can't be in two classes at once. |
| My booking says the room isn't set up for the instrument | Choose a room that is set up for that instrument (the room's types are shown to administrators on the Rooms page). |
| The Request Grade button asks for a reason | A short reason is required before the request can be sent. |
| The page looks out of date | Refresh the browser. If a warning asks about unsaved changes, choose **Stay and Keep Editing** to finish first. |

## 9. Frequently asked questions

**Why does one student appear twice on my calendar?**
Because they learn two instruments. Each instrument is its own class with its own time, so each gets its own card.

**Can a student's two instruments be taught by different teachers?**
Yes. Each instrument has its own teacher. You only see the class you teach.

**Do I need to mark attendance for every class?**
Yes, for every class you teach. For students on an attendance-based fee, the fee is worked out from the classes marked Present.

**Can I change a student's fee or my commission rate?**
No. Only administrators can do that.

**What happens to a grade request after I send it?**
It waits for the administrator. When it is approved, the grade changes. Approved and rejected requests are kept in **Archive → Notification Archive**.

**I rejected a clash request by mistake.**
Ask the other teacher to send the request again.
